Privacy Policy

Notte for iOS — last updated 10 September 2026

Notte does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to any third party.

How it works

Notte displays coding activity that already exists on your own Mac. A free open-source companion runs on that Mac and serves the numbers over your local network. Your iPhone reads them directly from your Mac. Nothing passes through the internet, and there is nothing for us to see.

What the Mac companion reads

It reads timestamps from Claude Code's own local session logs, and counts them. It never reads, stores or transmits your prompts, your code, your file names, your commands or Claude's replies. The results stay in a file on your Mac (~/.notte).

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Your pairing code, so you don't have to enter it again
  • Your Mac's address, if you typed one in manually
  • Which project you last had selected

These stay on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Photos

If you place your dial on a photo, the photo is read and combined with the dial on your device only. It is never uploaded. Notte asks for permission to add the finished image to your photo library, and only to add — it cannot read your library or see your other photos.

Local network

iOS will ask for permission to find devices on your local network. Notte uses it for exactly one thing: finding your Mac. It connects only to a Mac that answers with the pairing code you entered.

Children

Notte is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
